# ViViApp – User manual

*The teacher's mixer for headphone yoga classes · ViVi – Oltre il Benessere*

## What ViViApp does

ViViApp turns the tablet into a small mixing desk for headphone yoga classes. The teacher's voice (Bluetooth headset) and the music are mixed together and sent through the USB-C cable to the transmitter, which delivers them to the clients' wireless headphones.

- Headset microphone → ViViApp (voice cleaning, music, bells) → USB-C adapter → transmitter → clients' headphones
- The teacher also hears the music in her earpiece

## Before the first lesson

- Pair the Bluetooth headset in the tablet's Bluetooth settings, like any earphones.
- Use an "active" USB-C to jack adapter (with a built-in DAC). An adapter with charging passthrough also keeps the tablet charged.
- On first launch allow Microphone, Nearby devices and Notifications.
- Honor tablets: Settings → Battery → App launch → ViViApp → manage manually, and turn everything on, so the system does not close the app during the lesson.

## Starting a lesson

- Connect the cable to the transmitter and switch on the headset.
- Mixer tab → Start lesson. At the top you see the status (On air), the microphone and the output in use, and the battery.
- Pre-lesson check: a list of green/red ticks (cable, headset, voice, battery, music, microphone not muted) with a test beep you can hear in the clients' headphones.
- End lesson stops everything.

If the cable is unplugged, voice and music stop at once, so nothing comes out of the tablet's speakers. When you plug it back in, everything resumes (this can be turned off in Settings).

## Lesson screen

Mixer → Lesson screen shows only big buttons: play/pause music, microphone on/off, next track, bell, and next phase when a lesson plan is running. It also shows the phase countdown.

The screen starts locked, so an accidental touch changes nothing. Hold the padlock to unlock; tap it to lock again. Exit appears when unlocked.

## Voice

- Microphone: choose it with the buttons (Bluetooth headset, tablet microphone, USB).
- Voice volume: the only setting you need.
- Automatic clean voice: rumble filter, noise reduction, presence EQ, compressor, automatic level and limiter. It keeps the voice clear and steady whether she speaks softly or loudly.
- Microphone muted: silences the voice for the clients.

Bluetooth adds 100–200 ms of delay and phone-call quality: this is a limit of Bluetooth, not of the app. A wireless microphone with a USB-C receiver works much better and is recognised automatically.

If the headset drops out during the lesson, a red warning appears and the music continues for the clients. It reconnects by itself when the headset is back, or press Use tablet mic to carry on immediately.

## Music

- Play/Pause, Stop, Next.
- Lesson playlist: choose Whole library or one of your playlists; the numbered list shows the queue, tap ▶ to start a track.
- Repeat track (loop), Go to the next track.
- Crossfade between tracks: one track fades into the next (needs Go to the next track on and loop off).
- Fade in / fade out at start and pause.
- Automatic ducking: music lowers when she speaks and comes back up when she stops. Set how much it lowers; sensitivity and return time are in Settings.
- Music also in the teacher's earpiece: only she hears it, with its own volume; it follows fades but not ducking. Phone-call quality while the microphone is on.

Headset buttons: one press = play/pause, two presses = next track. With some headsets the button may not work while the microphone is in use.

## Bells

Singing bowl, gong, tingsha and chimes, sent into the clients' headphones to mark a change of pose or the end of savasana. They work while the lesson is on air; Bell volume sets their level. The sounds are generated by the app, so there are no copyright issues.

## Library and playlists

- Tracks: Import tracks from the tablet (mp3, wav, m4a, flac…). Under each downloaded track you see author, licence and source.
- Playlists: create one for each type of lesson, add tracks, reorder them with ↑ ↓, remove them. Load in mixer makes it the lesson queue.
- Recordings: the recorded lessons, to share or delete.

## Lesson plans

A lesson plan divides the lesson into timed phases (e.g. warm-up 10′, flow 30′, savasana 10′). Each phase can have its own playlist or keep the current music.

- Lesson plans tab → New lesson plan: name, phases with − / + minutes, music for each phase, bell at each change.
- Start it from the Lesson plans tab or from the Mixer. When a phase ends the bell rings and the music changes by itself; at the end the music fades out.
- Next phase skips ahead, Stop plan ends it.

## Finding music

- Find music: search free music on Openverse (Jamendo, Freesound, ccMixter, Wikimedia) and Internet Archive, with no registration. Only licences that allow use in paid lessons are shown. Search in English; ▶ Listen plays a preview from the tablet speaker (never on the cable, and not during a lesson); Download saves it to the Library with its credits. Some licences (CC BY) ask you to credit the author.
- Generate: instrumental tracks created online for free with ACE-Step. You need a free huggingface.co token (Settings → Access Tokens → Read). Pick one of the 20 styles for the branches of yoga, set the duration (up to 4 minutes) and press Generate track. The free quota is a few tracks a day.

## Recording the lesson

Mixer → Record lesson records what the clients hear (voice, music, bells) into an m4a file, about 1 MB per minute. Android asks each time for permission to "record or cast the screen": only the app's audio is recorded. Stop recording saves the file in Library → Recordings, where you can share it.

## Settings

- Language: English, Hrvatski, Italiano.
- Updates: when the app opens it offers new versions (never during a lesson); Check now checks immediately. The first time, Android asks to allow ViViApp to install apps.
- Test mode: to try the app without cable and headset (e.g. on the emulator). Turn it off for real lessons.
- Resume by itself when the cable is plugged back in.
- Headset buttons control the music.
- Call mode for the Bluetooth microphone: leave it on; turn it off only if music doesn't come out of the cable with the Bluetooth microphone.
- Advanced ducking: sensitivity and music return time.
- Backup: a single .zip with tracks, playlists, lesson plans and settings, to save and restore (e.g. when changing tablet).

## Troubleshooting

- "Connect the USB-C cable": the adapter is not recognised. Use an active adapter with a DAC, or try another one.
- The voice bar doesn't move: check the microphone chosen in the Voice section and that Microphone muted is off.
- Music doesn't come out of the cable with the Bluetooth microphone: turn off Call mode in Settings.
- The voice sounds crackly: it is often the Bluetooth signal; move closer to the tablet or use a wireless microphone with a USB-C receiver.
- The app closes during the lesson: check the Honor battery settings (Before the first lesson).
- The update won't install: allow "Install unknown apps" for ViViApp when Android asks.
- Generation fails: check the token with Test connection; the free quota may be used up for the day.
